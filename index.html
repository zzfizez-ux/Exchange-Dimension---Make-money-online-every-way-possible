04 / FAQ
How do I start?

Join our Discord server and open a request. We will review your request and explain the available options.

What is the fee?

The current service fee is 10% of the exchange amount. The final calculation is confirmed before you proceed.

Do you guarantee every transaction?

No. Requests are reviewed individually. We may decline requests that we cannot safely or legitimately support.

Do you bypass PayPal or other providers' rules?

No. Exchange Dimension does not provide a service intended to bypass payment-provider restrictions, identity checks, sanctions, or other applicable rules.
